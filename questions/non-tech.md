# Impact and Outcome

- What are 1–2 concrete outcomes this person delivered, and how did their work help the team/company?
- How would you describe the quality of their work (clarity, thoroughness, attention to detail)? Where does it shine, and where could it be stronger?
- Where do they take strong ownership, and what's one specific behavior or skill that would most increase their impact next cycle?

# Collaboration and Stakeholder Management

- How effective are they at collaborating across teams (alignment, expectations, feedback loops, unblocking others)?
- How clear, timely, and actionable is their communication (updates, decisions, documentation)? Any example?
